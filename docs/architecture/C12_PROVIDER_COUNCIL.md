# C12 — Provider Registry and Premium Model Council

Providers are dynamic adapters. Exact model IDs, catalog responses, capabilities, price, context, retention, privacy, health and benchmark results are versioned. The cockpit stores opaque secret references; raw keys are never returned or shown to models.

The council receives one evidence packet and begins with blind independent responses. Cross-critique follows only after commitments. Deterministic tests, citations, calibration and private task performance determine weights; equal vote is forbidden and correlated errors are penalized.

A minority report is mandatory. Hard-gate dissent can block a recommendation. The council may propose architecture or experiments but cannot change canon, Risk Kernel, broker routes, champion state or live locks.

Task-normal routing uses a small qualified subset. Critical gates can invoke a broader council. More models are not assumed to provide more independent evidence.
