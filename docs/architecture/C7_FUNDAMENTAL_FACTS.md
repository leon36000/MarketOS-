# C7 — Point-in-Time Fundamental Facts

The authoritative layer preserves the filed document, accession, acceptance/file time, first observation, durable persistence, strategy-availability time, reporting period, unit, dimensional context, taxonomy version, amendment chain and source hash.

The official filing remains the legal evidence. Structured tags are a machine-readable representation and are never treated as the sole authority when they conflict with the filing.

Three layers remain separate:

1. facts reported in the primary filing;
2. normalized mappings with explicit transformation and concept lineage;
3. analyst-derived estimates or interpretations.

Amendments and restatements create new versions. Historical contexts can access only versions available by their knowledge cutoff. The current cleaned database cannot replace a historical snapshot.

Period, instant, currency, scaling, sign, dimensions and consolidation scope are part of fact identity. Missing or conflicting contexts are quarantined rather than silently aggregated.
