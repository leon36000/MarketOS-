# C12 — Selective Temporal Memory

Raw episodes are immutable and include visible data, prompts, models, tools, decisions, outcomes, contradictions and hashes. Derived facts and lessons are separate versioned objects with valid time, knowledge time, source confidence and supersession.

A memory candidate enters quarantine before independent validation. Profit or loss alone never establishes lesson quality; attribution distinguishes signal, sizing, timing, cost, regime and luck and records counterfactual evidence.

Retrieval prioritizes exact IDs and authority/time filters before lexical, vector or graph expansion. Contradictions remain visible. Historical runs cannot retrieve future episodes, later embeddings, later summaries or corrected facts unavailable at the cutoff.

Negative results, incidents and open loops are retained. Deletion and retraction policies preserve audit evidence while respecting rights and privacy.
