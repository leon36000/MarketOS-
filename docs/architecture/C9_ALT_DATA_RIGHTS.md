# C9 — Alternative Data Rights and Evidence

Alternative data is admitted per product and use. Public visibility does not imply permission to retain, redistribute, embed, train, transfer to cloud or preserve after contract termination.

Each source records acquisition method, population/coverage, sampling and missingness, entity mapping, update delay, revisions, transformations, consent/provenance, legal policy, retention and deletion.

Features derived from web, social, geospatial, transaction, short-interest, insider, supply-chain or app data remain linked to the original policy and cannot outlive permitted source evidence unless the contract explicitly allows it.

Selection bias, coverage drift, platform changes, bot activity, stale identities, survivorship, seasonal effects and vendor backfills are monitored. A source whose population changes materially is treated as a new dataset version.

No alternative source is promoted because of an attractive historical correlation alone. It must survive point-in-time reconstruction, costs, capacity, legal use, independent corroboration and out-of-sample tests.
