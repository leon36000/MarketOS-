# C10 — Experiment Governance and Promotion

The ExperimentBook is append-only and links hypothesis, data, code, configuration, prompts, models, trials, metrics, costs, reviewers and decisions. Failed and abandoned experiments remain queryable.

Champion and challenger registries are separate. Candidate generation cannot read hidden holdouts, alter evaluation code or directly promote itself. Promotion requires complete evidence, independent review, minority findings, rollback and a later shadow/paper ladder.

A promoted strategy remains monitored for data drift, regime shift, calibration, costs, capacity and implementation divergence. Warning thresholds reduce exposure or fall back to shadow; hard violations retire or quarantine the strategy.

Profit and loss alone do not determine decision quality. Evaluation distinguishes signal, sizing, timing, execution, risk and luck.
