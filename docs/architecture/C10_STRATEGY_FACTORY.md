# C10 — Strategy Factory

A strategy is an immutable, versioned and falsifiable contract. It declares its economic mechanism, universe, point-in-time features, decision and sizing rules, execution assumptions, risk budget, abstention logic and known failure modes.

The factory supports directional, cross-sectional, factor, statistical-arbitrage, event-driven, volatility, relative-value, macro and execution strategies behind one typed interface. Strategy families are research taxonomies, not promises of edge.

Agents may propose hypotheses and candidates, but deterministic tools compile, validate and execute them. All parameter searches, prompts, code, data cutoffs, costs and rejected trials remain in an append-only ExperimentBook.

Every candidate competes against cash/NO_TRADE, random or simple rules and economically relevant established baselines. Complexity must provide stable marginal value after costs, capacity and uncertainty.
