# C11 — Multi-Fidelity Financial World Model Lab

Historical point-in-time replay is W0 and remains the authority. W1 bootstrap/statistical scenarios, W2 agent-based order books, W3 generative order books, W4 macro/event scenario graphs and W5 offline-RL environments are synthetic research instruments.

All synthetic outputs use an isolated namespace, explicit provenance and watermarks. They cannot repair, replace or augment historical observations invisibly. Every run pins source cutoffs, code, weights, prompts, seeds, interventions and hardware.

Common ports expose scenario, intervention, trajectory and metric contracts. ABIDES, JAX-LOB/JaxMARL-style environments and generative LOB candidates remain adapters behind those ports.

Worlds are useful for rare stress, intervention and counterfactual exploration. They are not evidence of actual fill probability, strategy edge or causal market structure until validated against real observations and held-out interventions.
