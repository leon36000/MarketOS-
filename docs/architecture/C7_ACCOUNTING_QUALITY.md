# C7 Accounting Quality

Quality is represented by transparent evidence flags with formulas, source facts, time scope, peers, thresholds, uncertainty and known failure cases.

Candidate families include accrual quality, cash/earnings divergence, working capital, capitalization policy, amendments, auditor/control changes, dilution, related parties, segment changes and reconciliation of non-standard reporting measures.

Original and amended facts remain separately queryable. Missing context or unexplained normalization causes degradation or quarantine rather than an invented value.
