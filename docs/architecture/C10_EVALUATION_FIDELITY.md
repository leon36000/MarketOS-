# C10 Evaluation Fidelity

Each result declares the detail level of its evidence, from idealized fixtures through richer event sequences to independently observed external outcomes.

A lower-detail level cannot validate behavior that depends on missing detail. Reports list unavailable mechanics, uncertainty and transfer limits.

Resource use, delay and operational friction are included as distributions rather than a single optimistic assumption.

Later observations are compared with earlier simulations. Divergence produces a new candidate version and does not rewrite earlier evidence.

A candidate advances only when conservative value, temporal stability and stress robustness remain positive. Synthetic fixtures cannot advance a candidate by themselves.
