# C14 — Operations, Alerts and Incidents

The Command Center correlates source data, feature, model, decision, risk, order, fill, reconciliation and outcome IDs. It displays health, freshness, latency, backlog, costs and current safety state.

Alerts are grouped and deduplicated but retain individual evidence. S0/S1 incidents require acknowledgement and escalation. Silences are time-bounded, authorized and visible.

Runbooks, safe-stop actions, timeline, affected components, evidence and postmortems are available from the incident view. Recovery of a dependency does not automatically restore financial authority.

Mobile supports viewing, acknowledgement and pre-authorized safe-stop actions only. Risk-increasing or secret/configuration actions require the full strongly authenticated interface.
