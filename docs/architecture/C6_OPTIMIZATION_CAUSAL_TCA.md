# C6 Optimization, Causal Analysis and TCA

Optimization inputs carry units, currencies, time, confidence and provenance. Feasibility precedes objective quality. Constraint violations, sensitivity and numerical status are retained. Infeasible or unstable problems produce NO_TRADE; constraints are never silently relaxed.

Causal analysis declares its graph, estimand, timing, assumptions and identification strategy. Negative controls, placebo tests and sensitivity to unobserved confounding are required. Unsupported results are associations and cannot use causal language.

Transaction-cost analysis is closed-loop: pre-transaction cost/fill distributions, in-flight divergence and limits, post-transaction attribution and independently validated recalibration. Attribution separates delay, spread, impact, slippage, fees, opportunity cost and fill-model error.

A mathematically optimal result that is operationally infeasible, uncalibrated or cost-dominated is rejected.
