# C2 L0/L1/L2/L3 Procurement Protocol

L0 supports broad baselines and slow-horizon research. L1 is the broad priority for initial US/Canada research and shadow/paper monitoring. L2 is acquired selectively for liquidity, capacity, auctions and impact. L3 is acquired selectively for queue and venue-specific microstructure.

Provider labels are insufficient: compare field-level schemas and perfectly aligned samples.

Every depth comparison uses the same instruments, dates, regimes, strategy logic, parameters and holdout policy. Measure changed decisions, fill uncertainty, slippage error, risk calibration, capacity, storage, compute, engineering complexity, rights and total cost.

L2 or L3 expands only when the conservative lower bound of incremental out-of-sample value remains positive after data, compute, storage, engineering and uncertainty costs.

Required cases include auctions, halt/resumption, high- and low-volatility sessions, fragmented trading, corporate-action boundaries and identifier changes. Reject unmatched message/depth requests, hidden survivorship filtering, unknown recovery semantics and performance comparisons made before semantic equivalence.
