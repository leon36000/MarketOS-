# C11 Research Ledger

Candidate research families:

- ABIDES and related agent-based interactive market simulations.
- JAX-LOB and vectorized limit-order-book environments.
- LOB-Bench and distribution/intervention realism metrics.
- conservative offline RL, fitted-Q evaluation, doubly robust estimation and behavior-policy support diagnostics.
- constrained policy optimization and safe-policy improvement literature.

Research results are hypotheses until reproduced on licensed point-in-time data. Synthetic benchmark performance cannot establish real-market fidelity or strategy value.
