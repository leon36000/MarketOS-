# C8 Source Ledger

Primary sources:

- Cboe market statistics and reference data: https://www.cboe.com/us/options/market_statistics/ and https://www.cboe.com/tradable_products/options/reference_data/
- Cboe historical option data: https://datashop.cboe.com/
- OCC information memos and contract adjustments: https://infomemo.theocc.com/infomemo/search-memo
- OPRA: https://www.opraplan.com/
- FIX Market Data standards: https://www.fixtrading.org/standards/
- ABIDES: https://github.com/abides-sim/abides
- JAX-LOB: https://github.com/KangOxford/jax-lob

These sources support contract and experiment design. They do not prove model fidelity, venue transferability or incremental value on the target system.
