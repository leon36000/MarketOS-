# C2 Procurement Questionnaire

Responses must be written and product-specific. Marketing pages do not close contractual gates.

## Product identity

Record contracting entity, exact product, upstream sources, venue and asset coverage, schema versions, history, delivery methods, support, service levels, change notice and contract dates.

## Temporal and reference semantics

Document native and normalized timestamps, clock source, sequence and recovery behavior, identifiers, listing continuity, inactive/delisted coverage, corrections, withdrawals, corporate-action versions and release vintages.

## Rights

Answer each permission separately: display, automated non-display use, users, devices, servers, sites, applications, storage, backup, retention, replay, derived features, cloud processing and regions, training, embeddings, model-output use, redistribution, audit, termination deletion and exit export.

## Costs

Itemize subscription, upstream pass-through fees, commitments, usage, connectivity, storage, transfer, compute, normalization, monitoring, support, reporting, migration and exit. Include validity dates and uncertainty ranges.

## Qualification samples

Deliver samples directly with hashes and schema documentation. Include active, inactive, renamed and corporate-action cases plus at least one corrected or withdrawn record. Depth and message samples must come from the same request and depth.

## Exit

Define export formats, deletion obligations, audit evidence, reproducibility rights and treatment of derived datasets, embeddings and trained models.

## Evidence boundary

Only written terms, directly delivered sample bytes and reproduced technical tests can close procurement gates. Unknown, ambiguous, verbal-only or expired answers remain `BLOCKED`.
