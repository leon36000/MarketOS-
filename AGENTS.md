# MARKET-OS — Agent Operating Rules

Agents may research, design, implement, test, or falsify. They may not silently change authority,
close a gate, promote a dependency, enable live trading, or claim profitability.

Every handoff must include:
- scope and non-goals;
- files changed;
- sources and assumptions;
- tests and raw results;
- unresolved risks and contradictions;
- rollback instructions;
- next exact action.
